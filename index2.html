Disadvantages of Old Oscilloscopes

  • Large, heavy, bulky
  • Complicated operation, trigger setting is a must
  • No digital data storage
  • Easily breaks

  • Time-consuming diagnostics – no direct data export possible
  • No analysis software – no modern interface for evaluation
  • Data cannot be recalled
  • Requires a high level of prior knowledge
